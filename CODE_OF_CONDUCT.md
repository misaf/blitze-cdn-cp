# Code of conduct

BlitzeCDN is built in public: issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions
are open to anyone. This page says how people taking part are expected to
treat each other, and what happens when they don't.

## Expected

- Be civil and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Keep to the topic of the issue or pull request.
- Give and accept review on the work itself, with reasons.
- Respect people's time: search before opening an issue, and include what was
  asked for in the template.
- Protect other people's information. Never post someone's credentials,
  private data or personal details, and remove your own before posting logs.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Discriminatory language or conduct of any kind.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their consent.
- Spam, trolling, and sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

This applies in every space the project runs: its issues, pull requests,
reviews, discussions, commit messages and security advisories, and wherever
someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems to GitHub. On the comment, issue or pull request, open
the `…` menu and choose **Report content**, or report the account from its
profile. The report goes privately to GitHub, which reviews it under its
[Community Guidelines](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/github-terms/github-community-guidelines).
Do not use a public issue for this. Security vulnerabilities go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
instead.

This project has no separate private channel for conduct reports, and the
maintainers do not see reports made to GitHub. They act on what happens in the
repository itself, as described below.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete comments, close or lock threads, and
block people from the repository, in proportion to what happened: from a
private note asking someone to stop, to a permanent block for harassment or
repeated violations. Maintainers who break these rules are held to them the
same way.
